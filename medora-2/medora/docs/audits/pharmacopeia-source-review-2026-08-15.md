# Pharmacopeia Source Review — 2026-08-15

## Official Egyptian source

The Egyptian Drug Authority page defines the Egyptian Pharmacopoeia as an official state-issued book with legal status. It describes monographs and pharmaceutical preparations, including definitions, specifications, identification tests, purity tests, assay methods, and preparation methods. The page states that the standards apply to human and veterinary medicines, and that the Egyptian Drug Authority's Permanent Committee for the Egyptian Pharmacopoeia issues and publishes it under the authority described in Article 19 of Law 151 of 2019.

Source: [Egyptian Drug Authority — Egyptian Pharmacopoeia](https://edaegypt.gov.eg/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A6%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B5%D8%B1%D9%8A/)

## Implementation boundary

MEDORA may store source metadata, edition, jurisdiction, effective date, licensed-access status, and verified monograph identifiers. It must not copy protected monograph text without a license, treat a pharmacopeia monograph as proof that a specific commercial product is registered in Egypt, or use a non-Egyptian reference to override the applicable national authority.
