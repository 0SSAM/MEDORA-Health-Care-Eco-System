
## Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA)

Source: https://edaegypt.gov.eg/en/about-us/about-us/
Accessed: 2026-08-15.

The EDA states that it regulates registration, circulation, and control of pharmaceutical products and relevant raw materials; implements laws and regulations for pharmaceutical products and medical devices; issues licenses for certain pharmaceutical establishments and operating permits within its statutory scope; licenses relevant private laboratories; examines medicines, biological products, cosmetics, medical devices, and laboratory/diagnostic reagents for quality, safety, efficacy, and conformity; manages release of imported products subject to its law; maintains an information base and exchanges information with local, regional, and international authorities; and evaluates pre-clinical and clinical medical research and related good-practice documentation and inspections.

Internal MEDORA implication: product master data, regulatory status, batch/lot traceability, expiry and recall controls, device and reagent catalog boundaries, laboratory workflows, and clinical-research records must remain configurable and evidence-based. The official source does not itself grant MEDORA an approval or integration entitlement; external EDA connectivity remains deferred until formal authorization, technical specifications, credentials, sandbox access, and acceptance evidence are supplied.

## UPA and GAHAR evidence

UPA public landing page: https://sc.upa.gov.eg/ — extracted 2026-08-15. The public page returned only a JavaScript-app shell in extraction; no public API contract or authorized integration specification was obtained. Therefore no UPA connector is claimed or activated.

GAHAR public site: https://www.gahar.gov.eg/en/ — extracted 2026-08-15. The site describes GAHAR as the General Authority for Healthcare Accreditation and Regulation and references its authority to accredit hospitals for implementation of the comprehensive health insurance system. This supports keeping accreditation evidence, quality indicators, corrective actions, controlled documents, and facility-scope records as internal readiness features; it does not constitute GAHAR accreditation of MEDORA.

EDA regulatory-guidelines index: https://edaegypt.gov.eg/en/the-regulatory-reference-of-the-egyptian-drug-authority-eda/regulatory-guidelines/ — extracted 2026-08-15. The index exposes distinct guidance areas for pharmaceutical policy and market access, biological/innovative products and clinical studies, pharmaceutical products, medical devices, pharmaceutical care, inspection of pharmaceutical institutions, drug control, and licensing of pharmaceutical institutions. Internal MEDORA implication: regulatory fields and workflows should be configurable by product class and facility type rather than hard-coded to one assumed authority process.
