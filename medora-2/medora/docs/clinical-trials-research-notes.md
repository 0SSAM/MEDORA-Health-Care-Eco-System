# Egypt Clinical-Trials Archive — Research Notes

## WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP)

- URL: https://www.who.int/tools/clinical-trials-registry-platform
- Accessed: 2026-08-14.
- WHO states that the ICTRP search portal is available at https://trialsearch.who.int/ and that other registries, including ClinicalTrials.gov and ICTRP Network primary registries, can be searched.
- WHO explains that ICTRP is not itself a clinical-trials registry and does not register trials; registration is handled by primary registries.
- WHO describes a clinical trial for registration as a prospective assignment of human participants or groups to one or more health-related interventions to evaluate health outcomes, covering Phase I–IV and interventions such as drugs, biologics, surgery, radiology, devices, behavioural treatments, and preventive care.
- WHO's stated purpose is to make a complete view of research accessible and strengthen research transparency. This makes ICTRP and its linked primary registries appropriate discovery sources for an Egypt-linked archive, but not a guarantee of complete coverage.

Source title: International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP), WHO.
