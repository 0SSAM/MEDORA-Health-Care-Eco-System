## Organization, branch, and jurisdiction

MEDORA enforces organizational scope at the server boundary. Regulated workflows resolve the active organization, branch, jurisdiction, and role from the authenticated server-side context rather than trusting client-provided scope.

Jurisdiction ID `0` is a valid legal scope when it is explicitly authorized by the applicable server-side policy and session context.

## AI safety boundary

The AI assistant is advisory-only. It may explain information, surface signals, and suggest next steps, but regulated mutations remain subject to explicit server-side authorization and workflow controls.

## Security and capture boundary

browser code is never claimed to prevent OS-level or physical capture absolutely. Client-side capture protections are treated as defense-in-depth rather than an absolute security boundary.

## Compliance statement

No legal or regulatory certification is claimed by the repository. Compliance readiness depends on the applicable jurisdiction, deployment controls, operational procedures, external approvals, and independent validation.
