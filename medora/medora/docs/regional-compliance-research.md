
## Saudi Arabia — initial official source

Source: [Saudi Food and Drug Authority](https://www.sfda.gov.sa/en). The official site presents SFDA as the authority covering food and drug safety and exposes regulated categories including Drugs, Medical Devices, and Cosmetics, as well as authority services for individuals, practitioners, and investors. The regional model should therefore support an authority profile with regulated categories, product registration/marketing authorization references, practitioner/investor workflows, source URL, version, effective date, and review owner. This is an architectural source, not a complete legal rule set or a certification.

## United Arab Emirates — official eInvoicing source

Source: [UAE Ministry of Finance eInvoicing](https://mof.gov.ae/en/about-us/initiatives/einvoicing/). The Ministry states that the official source for the UAE eInvoicing programme is its portal, defines an eInvoice as structured invoice data exchanged electronically and reported to the Federal Tax Authority, and describes a DCTCE flow involving accredited service providers, PINT AE, XML exchange, validation, message-level status, and Tax Data Document reporting. The country pack must therefore model structured-document formats, provider configuration, validation statuses, reporting statuses, timestamps, retry/idempotency, and legal-source versioning. This is not a substitute for UAE tax implementation approval.
