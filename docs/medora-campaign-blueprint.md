# MEDORA Health Care Eco System — Cinematic Campaign Blueprint

## Creative lock

**Brand:** MEDORA Health Care Eco System. **Master:** 150 seconds, 16:9. **Vertical cut:** 90 seconds, 9:16. **Language:** Modern Standard Arabic with a warm, confident male narrator. **Closing phrase:** «MEDORA | منظومة الرعاية الصحية المتكاملة». **Visual promise:** a realistic, connected healthcare operation in which workflows are visible, auditable, and human-led.

The film must show real professional behavior rather than a slideshow. It uses neutral urban healthcare settings, non-Gulf wardrobe, natural skin tones, deep teal and graphite with controlled amber highlights, practical light sources, macro inserts, motivated dolly and crane movement, realistic screen reflections, restrained data transitions, and precise sound design. The MEDORA mark is an interlocking pathway suggesting care, traceability, secure scope, and decision intelligence; it is not a literal shield or caduceus.

## Claim discipline

The film may present implemented capabilities as workflows: POS, barcode/Data Matrix payload handling, inventory and expiry visibility, batch traceability, procurement and balances, finance and cashier closing, CRM, HR and permissions, branch scope, audit trails, AI decision support with human approval, PDF/Excel reporting, and prepared integration boundaries. It must not claim government certification, GAHAR accreditation, ETA approval, live government connectivity, clinical outcomes, customer testimonials, ratings, or legal compliance unless separately verified and approved. GAHAR-oriented scenes are framed as readiness support and evidence organization.

## Audience narrative

| Audience | Screen action | Benefit expressed in voice |
|---|---|---|
| Pharmacy | Scan, search, confirm, print/share receipt | Faster dispensing and clearer stock visibility |
| Hospital/clinic | Prescription, stock, care queue, quality review | Connected care operations with auditable workflow |
| Distributor | Batch scan, warehouse movement, route handoff | Traceability from movement to delivery |
| Laboratory | Sample and reagent handoff | Reviewable operational cycle |
| Insurer/payer | Document reconciliation and status timeline | More structured records and fewer blind spots |
| Management | Branch dashboard, finance, HR, CRM, AI review | One decision surface with human control |
| Quality team | Evidence checklist and policy path | Preparation without unsupported certification claims |

## Film arc

Act I moves from disconnected work and delay into a single medicine package that becomes the visual thread. Act II follows the package and its records through pharmacy, warehouse, care, laboratory, payer, HR, CRM, finance, quality, and AI review. Act III resolves into branch isolation, auditability, coordinated operations, and the MEDORA mark.

## Sound direction

Original instrumental music only: modern cinematic hybrid, 80 BPM, restrained low pulse, warm piano harmonics, subtle strings, glass textures, scanner/Data Matrix chirps, controlled risers, and a clean final resolve. The narration remains intelligible above the music. Effects are motivated by actions on screen and never imply a fictional government connection.
