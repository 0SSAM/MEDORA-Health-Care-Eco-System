# MEDORA — Bilingual Live-Action Campaign Blueprint

**Campaign ID:** `medora-bilingual-live-action-2026-08-22`
**Working deliverables:** two separate 16:9 H.264/AAC edits, each approximately 60 seconds.
**Editorial basis:** user-authorized human-action footage only; independently authored MEDORA overlays, end card, narration, subtitles, music, and sound design.

> **Creative proposition:** Real care operations do not pause. MEDORA follows the people who inspect, scan, coordinate, review, and decide—using energy from their movement and the operational rhythm, never from a fictional approval or autonomous result.

## Production Rules

| Topic | Locked direction |
|---|---|
| Brand treatment | A small, high-contrast `MEDORA` wordmark remains in the upper-right title-safe area on all live-action shots. A full `MEDORA Health Care Eco System` end card closes both editions. The wordmark is independently rendered; no generated or inherited source logo is accepted. |
| Edit rhythm | 2.5–6.0 second shots, motivated match cuts on hand movement, scanner pulse, paper handoff, or person movement. No looping to extend footage, no static slideshow sections, and no inherited source transition cards. |
| Visual language | Deep navy and restrained teal, true skin tones, practical pharmacy/warehouse/office light, gentle handheld or crop-motion only where footage permits. Remove all source subtitle bands, UI, badges, labels, maps, and third-party text by selecting coherent safe regions rather than covering them with claims. |
| Sound | Original 120 BPM electronic/percussive instrumental with warm sub pulse, muted kick, hand percussion, short scan ticks, and understated risers. No orchestral/classical arrangement; preserve useful production ambience where clean. Duck music beneath narration. |
| Language editions | **Edition A:** English male narrator, Arabic burned-in subtitles. **Edition B:** Arabic male narrator, English burned-in subtitles. Each narration is independently timed; no automatic dubbing. |
| Claim boundary | Depict pack inspection, Data Matrix scanning, operating visibility, coordination, roles, quality focus, and human-reviewed decision support. Do not claim GAHAR or regulator approval, a live external integration, a completed e-invoice connection, clinical effectiveness, customer outcome, or autonomous AI action. |

## Quality Gate Before Rendering a Final MP4

1. Verify every selected source range at full size; remove any residual source subtitle, UI, product name, badge, logo, or sensitive data.
2. Verify the independent MEDORA wordmark appears clearly in each opening, middle, and closing visual sample without obstructing people or subtitles.
3. Confirm the English and Arabic narration scripts communicate only the statements in the verified-claims table.
4. Verify Arabic shaping, subtitle timing, and contrast on desktop and mobile-size review frames.
5. Verify a video stream and AAC audio stream with `ffprobe`; decode the whole file with `ffmpeg -v error`.
6. Measure and listen to opening, midpoint, and closing audio samples for narration audibility, no silence, clipping, or music dominance.

## Known Constraint

The current authorized source set can support an energetic recut, but it cannot honestly create all-new live-action coverage for every MEDORA audience without separately authorized original material or an authorized generator with visible usable free allocation. This blueprint therefore treats the new films as **source-limited live-action recuts**, not wholly newly filmed commercials.
