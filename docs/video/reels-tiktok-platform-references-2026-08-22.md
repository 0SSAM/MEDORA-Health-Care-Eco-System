# Reels and TikTok publication references — 2026-08-22

## Official platform requirements consulted

| Platform | Verified point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | Accepted Reel aspect ratios range from `1.91:1` to `9:16`; the stated minimum is `30 fps` and `720 px` resolution. Instagram also exposes an “Upload at highest quality” option in media-quality settings. | [Instagram Help Center — Reel size & aspect ratios](https://help.instagram.com/1038071743007909) |
| TikTok in-feed | TikTok recommends vertical `9:16` assets of at least `540×960 px`, accepts MP4/MOV among supported formats, permits up to 10 minutes, specifies a maximum 500 MB file size, and lists a minimum 516 kbps bitrate. The page also states that safe-zone requirements vary with format and caption length. | [TikTok for Business — In-Feed Auction Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads) |

These official pages were extracted on 2026-08-22. They describe upload/ad technical guidance, not a guarantee that a third-party mobile client will display all overlays identically after platform transcoding.
