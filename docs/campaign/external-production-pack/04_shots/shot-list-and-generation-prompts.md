# Shot List and External Video Prompts

## Locked global production specification

Use cinematic live-action healthcare technology realism, 16:9 for the 150-second master and an independently recomposed 9:16 variant for the 90-second cut. The world is a neutral international medical environment with deep medical navy, graphite, clean white, cool cyan data light, and restrained MEDORA teal. Use adult professionals only, consistent recurring characters, natural skin texture, practical soft lighting, realistic screens, shallow depth of field, and physically grounded cyan interface overlays. Every clip requires motivated camera movement and one clear human action.

| Element | Continuity lock |
|---|---|
| Lead pharmacist | Adult woman, charcoal medical coat, small teal badge accent, calm and precise. |
| Operations manager | Adult man, dark navy business-casual attire, measured analytical demeanor. |
| Hospital pharmacist | Adult man, white coat over muted blue scrubs, no patient details. |
| Distribution specialist | Adult woman, neutral high-visibility warehouse vest over dark clothing, handheld scanner. |
| MEDORA UI | Minimal bilingual-ready enterprise interface; navy side rail, white cards, cyan/teal highlight, one clear primary action. |
| Medicine carton | Generic, unbranded, sealed carton with a non-production Data Matrix visual; no real identifier. |

**Global exclusion for every generation:** no slideshow, no still-image montage, no watermarks, no invented logo, no visible patient information, no real medicines or brands, no fabricated certification, no dense unreadable dashboards, no Gulf dress, no political or religious stereotypes, no autonomous AI action.
