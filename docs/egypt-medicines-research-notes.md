## مصدر رسمي: قاعدة بحث هيئة الدواء المصرية

- URL: http://eservices.edaegypt.gov.eg/EDASearch/SearchRegDrugs.aspx
- Accessed: 2026-08-14.
- The public page identifies the Egyptian Drug Authority and states that it regulates, implements, and monitors the quality, efficacy, and safety of pharmaceutical preparations and medical supplies under its establishing law.
- Search categories visible on the page: Human Pharmaceutical, Biological, Herbal Medicine, Veterinary, Biocides, Cosmetics, and Complementary.
- Search fields visible: Trade Name, Registration Number, Applicant, and Generic Name. The page displays minimum-three-letter requirements for these fields and a CAPTCHA prompt.
- Coverage limitation: the page is an interactive search form rather than an openly downloadable full database. CAPTCHA and form behavior must be respected; records will only be collected through permitted public interactions, and no complete list will be claimed unless the public interface provides it.
- Contact information visible on the page: EDA public phone 15301 and info@edaegypt.gov.eg. These are source contact details, not project credentials.

Source title: Egyptian Drug Authority — Registered Drugs Search.
## صفحة قواعد البيانات الرسمية لهيئة الدواء المصرية

- URL: https://edaegypt.gov.eg/en/services/databases/
- Accessed: 2026-08-14.
- The page lists public EDA services including Released Batches, Herbal Monograph, EDDB (Egyptian Drug Database), Search Licences, Variation Tool, EDA SIMILARS, API Searching Tool, Biological Inspection, GCP Inspection, GSDP Inspection, and Egyptian Pharmacies.
- The EDDB link resolves to the interactive registered-drugs search page documented above.
- The page also exposes an API Searching Tool link to a Looker Studio report and separate pharmacy/inspection links. These are candidate sources for later verification, but they are not treated as downloadable datasets until their public fields and export permissions are confirmed.
- Coverage limitation: the EDA page is a directory of services, not a single complete Excel export. A complete Egyptian medicines workbook therefore cannot be claimed from this page alone.

Source title: EDA — Databases.
