# MEDORA Cinematic Advertising Film Blueprint

## Approved format

The campaign uses a 150-second cinematic horizontal master in 16:9, a 90-second vertical cut in 9:16, and short audience-specific edits. The spoken language is Modern Standard Arabic with a confident, warm male narrator. The closing phrase is: “MEDORA | منظومة الرعاية الصحية المتكاملة”.

## Creative promise

The film presents MEDORA as one connected operating system for healthcare: every product, prescription, transaction, employee, customer, branch, compliance event, and decision is connected through a secure, intelligent operational layer. The film demonstrates workflows rather than listing features.

## Visual language

Live-action cinematic realism, neutral urban healthcare environments, documentary-grade human behavior, natural skin tones, deep teal and graphite with controlled amber highlights, practical light sources, macro inserts, dolly and crane movement, shallow depth of field, crisp UI reflections, volumetric data transitions, restrained particles, and elegant match cuts. No Gulf dress styling, no generic slideshow montage, no impossible floating interfaces, no excessive sci-fi neon, and no unsupported regulatory claims.

## New symbol direction

Create a simple scalable MEDORA symbol that evolves beyond a conventional shield: an intelligent interlocking pathway forming a subtle medical cross and a secure orbital link, suggesting care, traceability, connected operations, and decision intelligence. The mark must work in one color, remain legible at small size, avoid literal caduceus imagery, avoid a generic shield silhouette, and pair with the existing MEDORA wordmark only where typography is rendered deterministically.

## Audience map

| Audience | Live-action focus | Distinct value proposition |
|---|---|---|
| Pharmacies | Counter sale, barcode/Data Matrix scan, inventory and expiry | Faster dispensing and safer stock visibility |
| Hospitals and clinics | Pharmacy workflow, patient-care coordination, quality review | Connected care operations with auditable processes |
| Distributors | Warehouse picking, batch/serial traceability, delivery handoff | End-to-end medicine movement visibility |
| Laboratories | Sample/result flow and stock controls | Organized workflows and reliable operational data |
| Insurers and payers | Claims/document review and reconciliation | Structured records and fewer operational blind spots |
| Management | Branch dashboard, finance, HR, CRM, AI recommendations | One decision surface across the organization |
| Quality and regulatory teams | Traceability, electronic invoicing, GAHAR readiness scenes | Evidence-oriented compliance preparation without legal overclaiming |

## Narrative arc

Act I shows disconnected work and the cost of delay. Act II follows one medicine package and one operational event through pharmacy, warehouse, care, finance, CRM, HR, electronic invoicing, GAHAR-oriented quality, and AI decision support. Act III resolves into one coordinated MEDORA operation and the new symbol.

## Sound

The score is a modern cinematic hybrid: low pulse, restrained strings, warm piano harmonics, subtle glass and scanner sound design, controlled risers, and a confident final resolve. The narrator remains clearly audible. Effects are synchronized to barcode scans, Data Matrix interpretation, warehouse motion, dashboard transitions, invoice confirmation, and the final mark reveal.

## Claim discipline

The film may show GAHAR-oriented workflows and readiness support but must not claim certification, government approval, ETA integration, or legal compliance unless separately verified and approved. AI is shown as decision support with human review, not autonomous clinical or financial authority.
